> How language models work, what they do well, and where they fail.

**Tracks:** AI at Work, Management, AI Leadership, Engineering, Safety & Governance · **Sources:** official OpenAI and Anthropic material, verified November 2026

## In this lesson

- Explain in plain words how a language model produces an answer
- Name the common failure modes: made-up facts, stale knowledge, missing context
- Review AI output with a simple checklist before you use it

## Why it matters

Every later lesson assumes you know what a model can and cannot do. Leaders need this to set realistic expectations, and builders need it to design checks around weak spots. Both academies start here for good reason.

## Try it

1. Pick a task you do every week, such as a status summary.
2. Ask a model to do it twice: once with no context, once with your notes pasted in.
3. Mark every claim in both answers as correct, wrong or unverifiable, and note what the context changed.

## Check yourself

1. Why can a model state something false with full confidence?
2. What is the difference between a model's training knowledge and the context you give it?
3. What three checks would you run before sending AI-written work to your manager?
