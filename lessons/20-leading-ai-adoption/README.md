## In this lesson

- Find and rank AI opportunities by value and feasibility
- Draft an AI strategy with governance and an adoption plan
- Measure adoption and impact after launch

## Why it matters

Tools are rarely the bottleneck; people, processes and priorities are. Both academies now offer courses built for leaders. This final lesson connects everything you learned to the decisions leaders make.

## Try it

1. List ten AI opportunities in your organization.
2. Score each on value, feasibility and risk, using what you learned in this course.
3. Write a one-page plan for the top two: owner, pilot scope, success metric, review date.

## Check yourself

1. Which of your top opportunities needs an agent, and which needs a workflow?
2. How will you measure adoption beyond logins?
3. What would make you stop or expand a pilot after 90 days?
