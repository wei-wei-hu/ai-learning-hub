# Lesson 20: Managing AI-Enabled Teams

> Redesign team workflows, set review standards and coach people as AI takes on more of the work.

**Tracks:** Management · **Sources:** official OpenAI and Anthropic material, verified November 2026

## In this lesson

- Map a team workflow and decide which steps go to AI, which stay with people, and where review happens
- Set quality standards for AI-assisted work that the whole team follows
- Measure whether AI is helping and coach the team through the change

## Why it matters

Most AI adoption happens one team at a time, and managers decide how it lands. A manager who redesigns the work, sets clear review standards and measures results gets far more from AI than one who only hands out tools. This lesson turns the earlier ideas into a plan for your own team.

## Try it

1. Pick one workflow your team runs every week, such as a weekly report or a customer reply process.
2. Map each step and mark it AI, person, or AI with human review; write the review checklist for the reviewed steps.
3. Run the new version for two weeks and compare time spent and error rate with the old one.

## Check yourself

1. Which steps in your team's work should never go to AI without review, and why?
2. What would a fair quality standard for AI-assisted work look like on your team?
3. How will you tell whether AI is helping your team, beyond how often people use it?
